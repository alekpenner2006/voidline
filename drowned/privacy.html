Drowned – Privacy Policy

Voidline Studios · Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

Drowned does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No chat or contact with other players.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No internet connection is used by the game.

Your saved progress

Your game progress (ship level, resources and captured islands) is saved only on your own device, in the game's private storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

App stores

When you download Drowned from an app store (Google Play, Microsoft Store or Apple App Store), that store may collect information under its own privacy policy. Voidline Studios does not receive personal information from the stores beyond the anonymous, aggregated statistics they show to all developers (for example, the number of installs).

Children

Drowned does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
